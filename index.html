About Vina

Certified lash artistry

Vina Lash Studio is a boutique lash and beauty studio in North Nanaimo, led by certified lash artist and trainer Vina Chen. We specialise in beautiful, long-lasting lash extensions — classic, hybrid and volume — along with lash lifts, brow and lash tinting, microblading and skincare, all in a calm, welcoming space designed around you.

Tailored to your eyes, every time.
